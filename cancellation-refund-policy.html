CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: 20 November 2026

1. Cancellation Request

Customers wishing to cancel their plot booking must submit a written cancellation request to Landmark Estates through the Company's authorised email or office, mentioning their name, project name, plot number and booking details.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility and applicable deductions, if any, will be determined based on the booking application, payment receipt, allotment letter, sale agreement and other applicable contractual documents, subject to applicable laws.

3. Cancellation Charges

Any cancellation charges or deductions shall be applied only where agreed in the relevant contractual documents and legally permissible.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

The standard refund processing period is approximately 45 to 60 working days from the date of receipt and acceptance of the cancellation request, subject to verification and completion of the required formalities. Mandatory statutory timelines shall prevail.

5. Refund Procedure

Approved refunds will generally be transferred to the original payment source or the customer's verified bank account after completion of the necessary documentation and approval procedures.

6. Company-Related Cancellations

If a booking is cancelled due to reasons attributable to the Company, refunds and any applicable compensation shall be handled in accordance with the signed agreement and applicable laws, including RERA provisions wherever applicable.
